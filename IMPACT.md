# CASTÚO-SYSTEM — Impact Model

## Purpose

This document translates technical work into a measurable social and operational impact model without treating an engineering capability as a demonstrated outcome.

## Impact chain

`problem → mechanism → expected outcome → indicator → evidence → review`

| Challenge | Technical mechanism | Expected outcome | Example indicator | Evidence status |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Irregular connectivity | Offline-first persistence and recovery | Better continuity during outages | Recovery success rate; unsynchronised events | Pending controlled execution |
| Fragmented field data | Stable event identity and evidence contracts | More traceable records | Provenance completeness | Partial / scope-specific |
| Vendor dependency | Export/replay/vendor-exit scenarios | Greater portability | Successful substitution/replay | Planned / pending |
| Weak auditability | Hashes, manifests, replay and review | Easier technical review | Reproducibility rate | Scope-specific |
| AI opacity | Intended-purpose, risk and human-oversight records | More reviewable AI use | Documented AI decision path | Developing |
| Digital exclusion | Accessible and offline-capable workflows | Better usability in constrained environments | Task completion by access mode | Pending field evidence |

## Current boundary

These are **impact hypotheses and measurement plans**, not claims of achieved social or economic impact.

No customer, field, clinical, financial or institutional outcome is represented here unless supported by dated evidence with a declared scope.

## Review cadence

Impact indicators should be reviewed when a controlled pilot or external reproduction creates measurable evidence. Results should remain linked to the exact product version, execution record and evidence package.
