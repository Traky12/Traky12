# CASTÚO-SYSTEM™ · Public technical evolution timeline

> **This public timeline is not a source of technical authority, operational
> status, production status or maturity-gate decisions. It is limited to
> publicly inspectable and scope-bound milestones.**

## How to read this timeline

Every milestone below can be checked by anyone in a public repository: its
creation, its first public commit, or a status recorded in that repository's own
documentation. Dates are taken from public repository metadata.

Public claims use the status taxonomy of the [profile README](README.md#public-status-taxonomy):
`CURRENT`, `CURRENT (PARTIAL)`, `TARGET`, `EXPERIMENTAL`, `PENDING` and `NOT_CLAIMED`.
Each repository's own documentation, not this timeline, defines the current
status of its contents.

Private work is not listed here. A private commit cannot be verified by a third
party, so it is not presented as public evidence.

## Future milestones

Future milestones (for example a Golden Path end-to-end run, deterministic
re-anchoring of evidence, an independent external replay, a pilot or a first
payment) are subject to separate evidence and human review. They are added here
only once a public, scope-bound artifact exists for them.

## What this timeline does not establish

A repository, commit, release or green workflow proves that an artifact exists.
It does not establish that a customer contract, grant, payment, certification,
legal conformity assessment, independent verification or production operation
exists. Those matters belong to their own documentary sources and must not be
inferred from public repository activity.

Technical evidence is kept separate from accounting, market value and investment
valuation. The official financial source remains authoritative for financial
figures.
