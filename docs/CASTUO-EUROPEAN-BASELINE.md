# CASTÚO-SYSTEM — European Regulatory Engineering Baseline

**Snapshot:** 2026-10-09  
**Status:** Engineering baseline · not legal certification

CASTÚO-SYSTEM applies a risk-based engineering approach to European regulatory readiness. Applicability is assessed by product, service, intended use, data, market activity and sector; this page does not claim legal compliance.

## Core baseline

**GDPR — Regulation (EU) 2016/679**  
Privacy by design/default, minimisation, security appropriate to risk, resilience, recovery and regular testing are treated as engineering controls where personal data is processed.

**AI Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/1689**  
AI systems are inventoried, their intended purpose and risk classification are recorded, and transparency/human-oversight controls are applied where applicable. As of 2 August 2026, covered transparency obligations are applicable; high-risk provisions remain subject to the staged dates defined by the current EU framework. 

**Cybersecurity**  
NIS2 is used as a proportional engineering benchmark for risk analysis, incident response, business continuity, supply-chain security, secure development, vulnerability handling, cryptography and training. Legal applicability is assessed separately.

**Cyber Resilience Act — Regulation (EU) 2024/2847**  
Products with digital elements are treated with secure-development, vulnerability-management, update and incident-readiness controls. CRA reporting obligations for actively exploited vulnerabilities and severe incidents apply from 11 September 2026 to manufacturers in scope.

**Data Act — Regulation (EU) 2023/2854**  
Where connected products or related services are involved, CASTÚO evaluates data access, portability, exportability and vendor-exit requirements.

**Accessibility — Directive (EU) 2019/882**  
Covered products and services are designed toward accessible interaction, documentation and error handling. Legal scope is assessed per service/product.

**Sector-specific controls**  
Radio/edge equipment may require RED/EMC/RoHS/WEEE assessment. Health-related work requires an explicit MDR and future EHDS applicability assessment.

## Engineering rule

`APPLICABILITY → REQUIREMENT → IMPLEMENTATION → TEST → EVIDENCE → REVIEW`

CASTÚO does not convert a checklist into a compliance claim.

## Evidence vocabulary

Use:

- `APPLICABILITY ASSESSED`
- `ENGINEERING BASELINE ADOPTED`
- `CONTROL IMPLEMENTED`
- `EVIDENCE AVAILABLE`
- `REVIEW PENDING`
- `EXTERNAL VALIDATION PENDING`
- `NOT CLAIMED`

Avoid unsupported labels such as `EU COMPLIANT`, `GDPR COMPLIANT`, `AI ACT COMPLIANT`, `CRA COMPLIANT` or `CE CERTIFIED`.

See the governance-plane baseline for the full applicability and resilience framework.
