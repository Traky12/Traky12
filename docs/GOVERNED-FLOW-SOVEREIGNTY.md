# Governed Flow — evidence sovereignty

> **SIMULATION · SYNTHETIC SOURCES · EXPERIMENTAL · NO PRODUCTION CLAIM**

This public illustration expresses a bounded governance concept for CASTÚO-SYSTEM: synthetic agricultural-domain signals pass through three increasingly strict policy gates, a simulated timed-release queue, and a SHA-256-linked synthetic evidence event. The conceptual brain is the destination of evidence for a responsible decision owner — not an automated decision engine.

![Governed Flow and evidence sovereignty](../assets/diagrams/governed-flow-sovereignty.svg)

## Flow

1. **Synthetic inputs:** 70 sources across seven simulated families. No real AI provider is connected.
2. **Policy gate 1 — grounding ≥ 0.18.**
3. **Policy gate 2 — grounding ≥ 0.36.**
4. **Policy gate 3 — grounding ≥ 0.52.**
5. **Simulated review queue:** a deterministic timed-release rule runs every 14 simulation steps. No human actor, approval, or rejection is modelled.
6. **Evidence event:** accepted flow items become synthetic records that can be chained with SHA-256. Simulated offline items remain pending until the simulated reconciliation path completes.
7. **Evidence destination:** the illustration ends at an evidence-sovereignty concept. It does not deliver an executable order or exercise authority over real operations.

## Reproducibility and boundaries

The seed identifies a reproducible run; it is not a governance authority. A given seed, simulation version, and policy produce the same simulated state under the stated model. The conservation invariant is:

`emitted = abstained + anchored + in_flight + simulated_review_queue + pending_offline`

Abstentions remain traceable and are not promoted automatically. Historical reconciliations are a subset of anchored records and must not be counted as a separate destination.

This public illustration **does not claim** live field telemetry, real cadastral data, connected model providers, field validation, human approval, operational deployment, production readiness, certification, or autonomous decision-making. Visual fidelity is not operational evidence.
