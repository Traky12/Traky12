CASTÚO-SYSTEM does not aim to compete directly with large industrial specialists in every technology domain.

Its focus is different:

> **To integrate capabilities that are usually distributed across separate systems into a modular, rural-first and evidence-driven digital infrastructure architecture.**

The long-term objective is to evolve from individual technical components into a coherent platform for intelligent and connected territories.

CASTÚO-SYSTEM no pretende competir directamente con grandes especialistas industriales en cada tecnología individual.

# Architecture

## Core layers

- Core Platform: central APIs, data, business logic and governance.
- Edge Layer: local execution, telemetry and offline operation.
- AI Layer: SABIONDA and AI-assisted decision support.
- Automation Layer: workflows and integrations.
- Observability Layer: metrics, health, logs and monitoring.
- Evidence Layer: traceability and technical validation.
- Digital Thread: lifecycle traceability from issue and implementation to evidence, deployment and release.
- Trust Layer: provenance, integrity and verifiable records.
- Experience Layer: applications, interfaces and visualization.

- Core Platform: APIs centrales, datos, lógica de negocio y gobernanza.
- Capa Edge: ejecución local, telemetría y operación offline.

# Technical Status

CASTÚO-SYSTEM follows a conservative capability model.

Detailed capability maturity is maintained using the N0–N6 capability maturity model and the ecosystem evidence registry.

- **Implemented** — functionality exists in the codebase.
- **Validated** — implementation has supporting tests or verification evidence.
- **Pilot** — capability is being evaluated in a real or controlled pilot.
- **Prepared** — architecture or technical foundations exist, but validation is incomplete.
- **Planned** — capability is part of the roadmap.

Technical claims are intended to remain aligned with repository evidence and documented maturity.

---

# Estado técnico

CASTÚO-SYSTEM utiliza un modelo de capacidades conservador.

La madurez detallada de las capacidades se mantiene mediante el modelo de madurez N0–N6 y el registro de evidencia del ecosistema.

- **Implemented** — la funcionalidad existe en el código.
- **Validated** — la implementación dispone de pruebas o evidencia de verificación.
- **Pilot** — la capacidad está siendo evaluada en un piloto real o controlado.
- **Prepared** — existen la arquitectura o las bases técnicas, pero la validación está incompleta.
- **Planned** — la capacidad forma parte de la hoja de ruta.

Las afirmaciones técnicas buscan mantenerse alineadas con la evidencia del repositorio y con la madurez documentada.
