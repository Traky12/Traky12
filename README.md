CASTÚO-SYSTEM is an **evidence-governed resilience architecture** for distributed operations that must remain inspectable when connectivity, infrastructure, providers or AI systems fail.

I design evidence-governed resilient systems for field operations that must continue when connectivity, infrastructure or AI services fail. CASTÚO-SYSTEM turns distributed operations into **inspectable, replayable and reviewable evidence**.

**Initial wedge / target market entry:** Evidence-Ready Field Operations.

Offline continuity · provenance · recovery · replay · assurance · bounded claims.

## Why CASTÚO?

Most systems optimize for availability. **CASTÚO optimizes for continuity, provenance, recovery, replay, reviewability and bounded claims.**

> The system does not become trustworthy because it claims resilience; it becomes more trustworthy when its behavior under failure can be inspected and reproduced.
