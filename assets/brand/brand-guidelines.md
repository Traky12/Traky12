# CASTÚO-SYSTEM™ Brand Guidelines v1.0.0

## Identity status

The CASTÚO-SYSTEM logo is an official identity asset only. It identifies the ecosystem and does not prove production, certification, customer adoption, revenue, legal compliance, operational readiness or federation.

```yaml
brand_asset:
  id: CASTUO-BRAND-LOGO-001
  version: 1.0.0
  source: official supplied asset
  status_claim: IDENTITY_ONLY
  evidence_status: NOT_APPLICABLE
  human_review: REQUIRED
```

## Approved files

`castuo-system-logo-master.png` preserves the supplied source bytes. JPG, square and horizontal files are approved derivatives generated from that source and listed in `checksums.sha256`.

## Usage rules

Preserve aspect ratio. Do not distort, recolor, crop away the identifying mark, place the logo over sensitive evidence, or use it to imply certification, partnership, regulatory approval or operational maturity. Maintain clear space of at least 25% of the displayed logo height and use a minimum display width of 180px where legibility matters.

## Public README block

Public READMEs may use the controlled block below. The status, claims and promotion state must continue to come from `castuo-evolution` and never from the logo itself.

```html
<!-- CASTUO:BRAND:START -->
<p align="center">
  <img src="https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Traky12/Traky12/main/assets/brand/castuo-system-logo-horizontal.jpg" alt="CASTÚO-SYSTEM official logo" width="520" />
</p>
<!-- CASTUO:BRAND:END -->
```

## Review boundary

Changes to the master, derivatives, checksums or controlled README block require a pull request, CODEOWNERS review and visual inspection. No brand change may alter control-plane state or promotion status.
