## Source handling rule

These notes preserve vendor-declared capabilities only. The competitive matrix must distinguish `1 = demonstrated`, `0 = not demonstrated`, `? = not verifiable` and `N/A = not comparable`. Vendor claims cannot be used as evidence that CASTÚO is inferior or superior, and no source here establishes market share, valuation, customer count or a Gartner ranking.
