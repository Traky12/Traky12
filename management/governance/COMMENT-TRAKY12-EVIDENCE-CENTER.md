## CASTÚO governance update — 2026-08-18

The Evidence Center issue is linked to the current Sovereign Evidence & Trust Control Plane plan. Promotion must distinguish N3 implemented, N4 integration-validated, N5 pilot-measured and N6 operational with observability/SLA.

**Required evidence per capability:** contract and commit lineage, reproducible tests, integration result, independent review where applicable, field protocol and signed KPIs for N5, and operational telemetry/SLO evidence for N6.

**Current boundary:** the dashboard and governance artifacts provide local contracts, replay, Incident Fabric, capability packs and gate readouts. They do not constitute field, production, commercial or federation evidence.

**Owner:** Evidence Center maintainer with the relevant capability owner and reviewer.

**Rollback:** return a capability to the last supported maturity state when evidence is incomplete, replay diverges or a mandatory gate is zero.

**Claim rule:** planned and implemented surfaces remain separate from validated, pilot or operational claims.
