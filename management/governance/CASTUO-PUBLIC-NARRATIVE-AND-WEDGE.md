# CASTÚO-SYSTEM Public Narrative and Commercial Wedge

## Canonical positioning

**CASTÚO-SYSTEM is an evidence-governed control plane for distributed operations and AI-enabled systems, with Evidence-Ready Field Operations as its first commercial wedge.**

This positioning describes the intended product architecture and entry point. It does not claim production availability, customer adoption, certification, autonomous authority, recurring revenue or field validation unless the corresponding evidence gate is closed.

## Professional identity

The canonical profile title is:

> **Founder & Lead Architect of CASTÚO-SYSTEM · Systems Architect · Evidence Engineer · AI Governance & Assurance**

A shorter variant for compact profile surfaces is:

> **Founder & Lead Architect at CASTÚO-SYSTEM | Systems Architect, Evidence Engineer, AI Governance & Assurance**

## First commercial wedge

The first commercial wedge is **Evidence-Ready Field Operations**. Its plain-language journey is:

```text
Operate offline → preserve evidence → review → export
```

The bounded product journey is:

```text
Create organisation → register an operation → continue through connectivity loss
→ synchronise → review evidence → export a report
```

This is an entry product, not a claim that the full ecosystem is production-ready or commercially validated. A paid pilot, KPI achievement, renewal and repeated operation require separate gates.

## Public baseline role

`Traky12` is the public baseline and read-model:

```text
public baseline → index → claims → evidence links → boundaries
```

It is not the implementation authority. The control plane remains governed by repository roles and the sovereign reference architecture. Public summaries must link to evidence and preserve `CURRENT`, `TARGET`, `EXPERIMENTAL`, `PENDING` and `NOT_CLAIMED` semantics.

## Confidence progression

The public confidence sequence is:

```text
evidence-scoped → independently reproduced → field validated → commercially validated
```

The sequence is descriptive, not promotional. `E0`–`E6` evidence strength and `N1`–`N6` maturity ceilings remain authoritative. A capability is not a claim, and a claim is not a competitive advantage.

## Prohibited shortcuts

The profile must not claim that CASTÚO is better than named competitors, best-in-class, commercially superior, production-proven, field-validated, independently certified or revenue-generating without a dated, scope-bound evidence passport. The profile must not present repository count as product count or architecture breadth as market proof.
